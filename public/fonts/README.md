Five additional unmodified families are bundled from the same pinned official Google Fonts commit `47d54ca1ebb14fd864695f1d39dc734f39017c33`. Barlow Condensed Bold and IBM Plex Mono Medium are fixed-weight faces; the requested weight is constrained to the installed face rather than relying on synthetic bold. Lora supports weights 400–700 and Fraunces supports 100–900. Italic styling may be synthesized consistently by the same browser renderer used for preview and export.
