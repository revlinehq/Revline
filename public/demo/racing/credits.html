Race day sample

This 13.2-second sample combines pit-stop practice and warm-up footage from separate events. It is an independent editing demonstration; no affiliation with or endorsement by the depicted teams, drivers, sponsors, event organizers, or footage authors is implied.

Changes made by Revline

The sources were trimmed, reordered into six shots, and converted to 1280 × 720 at 30 frames per second. Pit-stop shots were cropped. Original audio was extracted into separate AAC files, with short edge fades in the edit. Three title graphics and two gentle wheel-centered zooms were added in the editable timeline. No synthetic speech, music, or replacement sound was added.

The landing-page version joins the same six shots into a muted 13.2-second video, with three titles and one subtle wheel-change zoom in the preview.
