- Web app: Campaigns (search, Popular now, More campaigns, All / Mine), Campaign detail with Save and a "Make clips" dialog that starts a project attached to the campaign, and Stats (estimated earnings, totals, your campaigns). Why: people who clip for money need to find a paying campaign, start clipping it and see what their posts earn.
- Backend: `campaigns:lastUpdated` and `posting:earnings` queries, and campaign search falls back to word search when the meaning search finds nothing. Why: the pages need the reader's last run and a per-campaign estimate, and a deployment whose campaigns have no embeddings returned no results.
