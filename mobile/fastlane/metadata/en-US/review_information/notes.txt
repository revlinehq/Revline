The owner must provide a dedicated reviewer account and access instructions before submitting for review. Do not use the owner's personal account.

Before the first media import or edit request, an explicit disclosure names the AI recipients and what is shared. Account settings can withdraw that permission. The flow explains that App Store subscriptions must be cancelled separately and links to Apple subscription settings.

Apple displays the local price and trial eligibility before purchase. Restore Purchases, Privacy, Terms and Apple's standard EULA are present. Existing supported Stripe or complimentary subscribers bypass the paywall.
