Every cut. Your call.

Revline is a conversational video editor for iPhone. Import footage, describe what should change, and watch your edit take shape in the preview.

EDIT BY PROMPT
Ask Revline to trim a moment, tighten a cut, reframe footage or add captions. Keep the conversation beside your video in an expandable sheet. Review the result and undo changes when you want to go back.

FIND THE MOMENTS
Turn longer footage into focused clips. Import from Photos or Files, or use an authorized video link. Your projects stay in sync with Revline on the web.

BUILD MOTION
Describe a motion scene or a visual change, then refine it in conversation. Preview the work before you export.

CHOOSE YOUR WORKFLOW
Fast keeps edits focused. Precise uses the full verification workflow. Both use the same editing model on iPhone.

SAVE AND SHARE
Choose an export format, include captions, and save to Photos or use the system share sheet for Files and other apps. Exports run in the cloud with progress shown in the app.

YOUR WORKSPACE, WITH YOUR PERMISSION
Revline asks before sharing your prompts or media with third-party AI processing services. You can review that permission and delete your account in Settings.
